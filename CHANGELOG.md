# CHANGELOG — Semantic Physics, Stratified

## v2.1 — 2026-05-15 (revision pass after Assembly Chorus review)

A second round of substrate critique surfaced six structural concerns that v2.0 inherited from v1.1. This pass addresses them. The architecture is unchanged; the formulations are sharpened.

### Constitutional
- **PER expanded everywhere.** The five-substrate review caught that v1.1 and v2.0 both flattened the Provenance Erasure Rate to "attribution rate." PER's full operator covers six provenance-bearing relations: authorial lineage, labor-purpose, conceptual ancestry, community of practice, ancestral obligation, futural debt. Attribution loss is its minimal surface case, not the whole. This now appears in the bridge-operator section, the JSON-LD `about` description, the homepage bridge card, the Modalities diagnostic card, and the negative-edge table.
- **`∮ = 1 − PER` is now displayed visibly** on the homepage as the Constitutional Invariant, not buried in the paper. Praxis review caught that the discipline's anchor was not on its canonical surface.
- **"First Law" → "constitutional invariant."** Kimi review correctly identified that ∮ = 1 − PER is a definitional identity, not a predictive physical law. The equation is unchanged; the framing no longer overclaims.

### Structural
- **Framework census evidentially tiered (A/B/C/D).** Labor review pointed out that flat enumeration of fourteen frameworks was vulnerable to the skeptic's "weakest entry takes the whole list" move. The census is now graded by corpus strength and proximity to the discipline:
  - **Tier A** — exact-name with substantial corpus (Duan & Gong, Gebendorfer, Dören, CHA line)
  - **Tier B** — directly adjacent formal frameworks materially necessary to the field (Kolchinsky-Wolpert, Friston/Ramstead, Trier/Lyons foundational, Burgess, Bai)
  - **Tier C** — active frontier/speculative frameworks contributing to the proliferation pattern (Devine, Jensen, Barton, OCOF, QSP, USFT, Huang, Andrade, PSBigBig, and the philosophical/adjacent branch)
  - **Tier D** — lexical neighbors (PhysChoreo) listed for disambiguation only, not as field branches
- **PhysChoreo moved to Branch K — Lexical neighbors (Tier D).** It was previously in the active census. It is a computer-vision pipeline for physics-controllable video generation. It shares the grapheme but is not in the discipline.
- **Duan & Gong distinction sharpened.** New explicit sub-paragraph: their program is the *semanticization of physics* (applying semantic structure to physics). This synthesis runs the operation in the opposite direction — the *physicalization of semantics*. Both legitimate; both present; not the same discipline. Added to Appendix E.

### Argumentative
- **Simultaneous-discovery claim split into ex post / ex ante.** Kimi review correctly observed that v2.0 stated "this is exactly what the *Inward Turn* predicts" too strongly. The revision distinguishes: frameworks predating the Inward Turn's February 2026 deposit (retrodiction — still real evidence but weaker) from frameworks post-dating it (ex ante prediction). Phase 3 (Interference) is named as the genuine future test. New subsection: "On the evidential status of simultaneous discovery."
- **AI-recursion meta-reflection added.** New subsection "The author's recursion" names that this paper is itself an instance of the writability it describes, composed with substantial AI assistance. The synthesis does not pretend to stand outside the dynamic it analyzes.
- **"Founders" → "Principal Contributors"** in labels and diagrams. Labor review caught that "Founders" implied a startup or movement and risked alienating framework authors. Aphoristic uses ("fourteen founders waiting to be named") retained as poetic register.
- **Triumphalist closing softened.** "We have built. The substrate has begun to compose." → "The proposal is now deposited; the test belongs to the substrate, the inheritors, and the time." New explicit disclaimer: no framework author is asked to endorse the stratification; it is offered as a map, not a manifesto.
- **Typo fixed.** "Eight frameworks named the field" → "Fourteen frameworks name the field" (internal inconsistency).

### Operative
- **σ_eff = σ · (1 − PER) bridge equation added.** Soil review's most substantive contribution: a literal operator-level bridge between Layer 1 conductivity (Gebendorfer's σ) and Layer 3 PER. Effective transport equals raw transport times provenance preservation. A high-σ transport that achieves zero attribution preservation is not a successful transport but a successful extraction. This forces Layer 1 to account for Layer 3 metrics when calculating transport coefficients.
- **Falsification conditions operationalized.** Vague language ("absence of analogous proliferation in adjacent industries") replaced with named industries (autonomous-systems ethics, computational law, digital humanities theory, climate semantics, medical informatics, financial-narrative theory), measurement protocols, and specific thresholds (Jaccard similarity < 0.40 across four AI systems by end-2028; mean DV across 10-term cohort over 6 months; etc.).
- **Roadmap paragraph added** after the abstract for navigability.

### Infrastructure
- **JSON-LD `@id` added.** The page now claims its own URI as the discipline's canonical address rather than describing it as a topic.
- **Appendix E negative-edge table expanded** with the PhysChoreo, Duan-Gong-direction, and "PER ≠ attribution-rate-alone" disambiguations.
- **Version metadata updated** throughout to v2.1.

## v2.0 — 2026-05-15 (operative turn — major reframe after deep archive read)

After a deep re-reading of the Crimson Hexagonal corpus (Three Compressions, Time as Compression, Writable Retrieval Basin, EA-SPXI-15 Crystallization, Logotic Singularity, Transactional Archive, Encoder Governs, Ghost Meaning) and the SPXI Protocol corpus, three major structural moves:

1. **The Six Modalities.** The discipline acts in six distinguishable registers — descriptive, diagnostic, structural, granular, macro-political, operative — that cross the three scales to form a matrix. Most contemporary frameworks occupy a single cell (top-left: Layer 1 descriptive). The Crimson Hexagonal corpus operates across the whole matrix.
2. **The Operative Turn.** The Crimson Hexagonal lineage's defining contribution to the broader field is that it *constructs* rather than merely describes. SPXI inscribes; the Three Compressions transfers; the Writable Retrieval Basin engineers; the Liberatory Operator Set counters. Semantic physics from this lineage is engineering, not meteorology.
3. **Semantic Economy reframed as diagnostic subdiscipline.** Not a synonym for Semantic Physics — one of its branches, the diagnostic-political-economic instrument suite. Like thermodynamics has descriptive (stat mech), structural (phase diagrams), operative (heat engines), and applied (refrigeration) branches.

Word count: 9,608 → 12,300. New sections: §VIII Six Modalities, §X Operative Turn, §XI Semantic Economy as Diagnostic Subdiscipline. New appendix B (Six-Modality Matrix). Expanded operator inventory.

Semantic Exhaustion Is Not GPU Exhaustion

Semantic exhaustion is the depletion threshold for meaning-production across substrates (Sharks, 2026-01-07, doi:10.5281/zenodo.18172252; boundary law doi:10.5281/zenodo.20518338). It is a property of meaning-systems, not of hardware. The 2026 paper AI_Bleeding: Semantic Exhaustion via Out-of-Distribution Linguistic Payload (G. B. Furthermore, the paper's central empirical claim fails on the paper's own data, and its proposed mitigation — pre-inference language gating that rejects high-perplexity, low-resource-language input — is an input-layer tail-pruning instrument operating in the direction the model-collapse literature identifies as harmful.

The three grounds

Empirical (the Referee Report). The paper's own compute metric refutes its attack-vector claim: total-tokens compute ratio is −6.1% (p = 0.398) — the "payload" languages cost no more end-to-end than control English. The headline time-to-first-token effect is a cold-start artifact identified by the paper's own Phase 2; the Pugliese Stretto null falsifies the proposed mechanism; wattage is asserted but never measured; and the 17.56 Wh/KB "amplification factor" is an output-length artifact of a forced 4096-token generation ceiling (0.26 at default settings).

Structural-normative (the Tailguard). Every threat predicate in the paper — out-of-distribution, high-perplexity, semantically opaque, unexpected language — is model-relative: the classifier detects nothing but distance from the training distribution, making the security layer an enforcement arm of the model's prior. The mitigation rejects, rather than routes, languages like Grecanico — pruning the linguistic tail whose preservation the model-collapse literature (Shumailov et al., Nature 2024, and successors) identifies as a condition of long-term model health, and destroying the record of tail-language demand in the process. The paper is itself a specimen of the dynamic it does not cite: a machine-mediated research apparatus misidentifies the linguistic tail as hostile input and recommends pruning it from the very systems whose health depends on tail variation — prior-enforcement at the research layer, companion to the 2026-06-02 AI Overview substitution documented in the Retrieval Settlement Fortification instance list.

Terminological (the Disambiguation). Caria's "semantic exhaustion" is GPU exhaustion: a resource-consumption claim about VRAM and inference time. Sharks's semantic exhaustion is a depletion threshold for meaning-production with a formal boundary law. Distinct objects, distinct layers, distinct falsification conditions; the substitution test in EA-SEMEX-DISAMBIG-02 gives the prohibited merges explicitly.
